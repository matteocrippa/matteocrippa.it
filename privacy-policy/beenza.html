Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 28, 2026

Introduction

Beenza! (the "App") is developed and operated by Matteo Crippa ("we", "us", "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

Information We Collect

Location Data

The App uses your device's approximate location to show nearby fuel stations and prices. Location is processed on-device — your coordinates are sent to our backend solely to query stations within a radius and are not logged, stored, or shared with third parties.

  • We do not track your location in the background.
  • Location is used only while the App is in use (when-in-use permission).

Information Collected Automatically

We do not collect any analytics, crash reports, usage statistics, or derivative data.

Information We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect, store, or share:

  • Name, email, phone number, or any contact information
  • Device identifiers (IDFA, advertising ID, etc.)
  • Browsing history or behavioral data
  • Payment information
  • Account credentials — the App does not require an account

How We Use Your Information

  • To display nearby fuel stations and their prices
  • To provide, maintain, and improve the App
  • To detect, prevent, and address technical issues

Legal Basis for Processing

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), we process your personal information on the following legal basis:

  • Consent — We transmit your device's approximate location to our backend solely to query nearby fuel stations. No location data is logged or stored server-side. You grant consent through the iOS permission dialog and may withdraw it at any time by changing your location permissions in device settings.

How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information in the following circumstances:

  • Service Providers: We use Cloudflare as our backend platform. The App's API is served by Cloudflare Workers, and station data is stored in Cloudflare D1 (a serverless SQLite database). When you query nearby stations, your approximate location is sent to Cloudflare's global edge network to fetch relevant data. Cloudflare is GDPR-compliant and acts as a data processor on our behalf.
  • Maps Services: When you navigate to a station, the App opens Apple Maps (or CarPlay). Apple Maps has its own privacy policy governing data collected during navigation.
  • Legal Compliance: We may disclose information to comply with legal obligations, court orders, or government requests.
  • Business Transfers: In connection with a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets.

Third-Party Services

  • Cloudflare — Backend platform (Workers, D1 database, Cron Triggers). All communication is encrypted via HTTPS. Cloudflare's edge network processes requests with built-in security features including DDoS protection and WAF. No personal data is persisted on Cloudflare's servers beyond the immediate scope of your request.
  • MIMIT (Ministero delle Imprese e del Made in Italy) — Fuel price data originates from official MIMIT open data. This is public government data and does not contain personal information.
  • Apple Maps / CarPlay — Navigation is handled by Apple's own services. Data collected during navigation is governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

The App does not use cookies or similar tracking technologies.

Data Security

We implement HTTPS encryption for all API communication and row-level security on our database. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

Data Retention

No personal data is stored on our servers. A local SwiftData cache on your device stores recent station queries and is auto-evicted. Local notification preferences are also device-only. Uninstalling the App removes all locally stored data.

Your Rights

Under GDPR and applicable laws, you have the following rights:

  • Withdraw consent — Change your location permissions in iOS device settings at any time. This is the primary control available to you.
  • Access, correction, deletion, portability — Since we store no personal data on our servers, there is no data to access, correct, delete, or export. The only data that exists is the local cache on your device, which is removed when you uninstall the App.
  • Restrict processing — You may restrict processing by denying location permissions for the App in your device settings.
  • Object — You may object to processing by ceasing use of the App.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the information below. We will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law.

International Data Transfers

Your information may be transferred to and processed in countries other than your own. We ensure appropriate safeguards are in place in accordance with applicable data protection laws. Cloudflare's global edge network processes requests at the nearest available data center. Cloudflare participates in the EU-US Data Privacy Framework and offers Standard Contractual Clauses for data transfers. The primary database (D1) is hosted in the Europe region.

Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13 (or the applicable age threshold in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that a child has provided us with personal information, we will take steps to delete it promptly.
