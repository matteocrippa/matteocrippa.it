Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 28, 2026

Introduction

RoadQuest (the "App") is developed and operated by Matteo Crippa ("we", "us", "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

The App is a gamified CarPlay travel companion that automates the "Window Scavenger Hunt" game. Privacy is a core design principle — the App is fully offline, requires no account, and collects no personal data.

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information. The App is designed to be entirely offline and anonymous:

  • No accounts: The App does not require sign-in, registration, or any account creation
  • No networking: The App makes no network requests of any kind
  • No location data: The App does not access your device's GPS or location services
  • No analytics: We do not collect usage statistics, crash reports, or telemetry
  • No advertising: The App contains no advertising SDKs

Information We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect, store, or share:

  • Name, email, phone number, or any contact information
  • Device identifiers (IDFA, advertising ID, vendor ID, etc.)
  • Location data, GPS coordinates, or travel routes
  • Browsing history or behavioral data
  • Payment or financial information
  • Account credentials
  • Photos, contacts, calendar, or media
  • Health or biometric data
  • Usage analytics, crash reports, or telemetry

How We Use Your Information

Since we collect no information, there is no information to use. All game state (player names, scores, session history) is stored locally on your device using SwiftData and is never transmitted.

Legal Basis for Processing

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), we do not process any personal data. All app data is created and stored locally on your device at your own direction. No consent is required because no personal data is collected, transmitted, or processed by us.

How We Share Your Information

We do not share your personal information with anyone because we do not collect any.

  • Service Providers: The App has no backend services, no APIs, and no service providers.
  • Legal Compliance: In the unlikely event of a legal request, we have no data to disclose.
  • Business Transfers: In the unlikely event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, no user data would be transferred because no user data is collected.

Third-Party Services

The App contains no third-party SDKs, frameworks, or services. Specifically:

  • No analytics (Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Amplitude, Mixpanel, etc.)
  • No crash reporting (Sentry, Crashlytics, etc.)
  • No advertising (AdMob, etc.)
  • No social media SDKs
  • No payment processors
  • No push notification services
  • No remote configuration or A/B testing frameworks

Apple Services: The App integrates with Apple CarPlay and AVSpeechSynthesizer for voice announcements. These are system-level APIs that operate entirely on-device. No data is sent to Apple as a result of using the App.

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

The App does not use cookies or any form of tracking technology. There are no web views, no cookie storage, and no fingerprinting mechanisms.

Data Security

All app data (player names, scores, session history) is stored locally on your device using SwiftData (Apple's on-device persistence framework). The App makes no network connections, so there is no data in transit to intercept.

  • On-device processing: All game logic runs locally
  • No network requests: The App never connects to the internet
  • App Sandbox: iOS sandboxing prevents unauthorized access by other applications
  • Data minimization: Only the minimum data necessary for gameplay (player names, scores) is stored

No system is 100% secure, but because no personal data exists and no network requests are made, there is no attack surface.

Data Retention

All app data is stored locally on your device and is removed when you uninstall the App:

DataStorageRetention
Player profilesLocal SwiftDataUntil deleted by user or app is uninstalled
Session historyLocal SwiftDataUntil deleted by user or app is uninstalled

Your Rights

Under GDPR and applicable privacy laws, you have the following rights:

  • Access, correction, deletion, portability — All your data resides on your device. You can delete it at any time through the app's Hall of Fame or by uninstalling the app.
  • Restrict processing — Since no data is processed by us, there is nothing to restrict.
  • Object — You may object to processing by ceasing use of the App. No data will have been collected.
  • Lodge a complaint — You have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority if you believe your rights have been violated.

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the information below. We will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law.

International Data Transfers

No personal data is transferred across borders because no personal data leaves your device. The App makes no network requests.

Children's Privacy

The App is not intended for children under 13 (or the applicable age threshold in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect any personal information from children — in fact, we do not collect personal information from any user regardless of age.
