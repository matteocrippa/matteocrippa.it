Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 31, 2026

Introduction

E2Control (the "App") is developed and operated by Matteo Crippa ("we", "us", "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

The App is a remote control and media player for Enigma2-based satellite and cable receivers (for example Dreambox or VU+ devices) that expose the OpenWebIf HTTP API. Privacy is a core design principle — the App collects no personal data and requires no account.

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

Information We Collect

We do not collect any personal information. The App is designed to be entirely private and anonymous:

  • No accounts: The App does not require sign-in, registration, or any account creation
  • No telemetry: We do not collect usage statistics, crash reports, or analytics
  • No advertising: The App contains no advertising SDKs
  • No third-party data processors: The App has no backend servers of ours — all traffic goes directly from your device to your own receiver

Your Receiver and Local Network

To function, the App connects to a receiver you configure yourself — your own Enigma2 device on your local network — using the standard OpenWebIf HTTP API:

  • You provide the receiver's IP address, port, and optional credentials
  • All communication happens directly between your device and your receiver over your local network
  • Traffic may transit your home router, but it never passes through any server we operate or control
  • We do not see, log, or store the content of these requests

The App is not affiliated with VideoLAN, VLC, or any receiver manufacturer. "Enigma2" and "OpenWebIf" are terms used to describe the devices and interfaces the App connects to.

Data Stored on Your Device

Some data is stored locally on your device to make the App usable:

  • Receiver configuration (name, host, port) is stored on-device
  • Credentials (username and password, if you use authentication) are stored securely in the iOS Keychain and are never transmitted to us
  • Channel cache is stored on-device to speed up browsing

All of this data can be removed by deleting the App or the individual devices from the device list.

Third-Party Software

The App includes VLCKit, an open-source video playback library (GNU LGPL-2.1-or-later) that plays video streams on your device. VLCKit operates entirely on-device and does not transmit any data to us or to VideoLAN. Its license and source are acknowledged within the App under Settings → Licenses.

How We Share Your Information

We do not share your personal information with anyone because we do not collect any.

  • Service Providers: There are no backend services, no APIs, and no service providers.
  • Legal Compliance: In the unlikely event of a legal request, we have no data to disclose.
  • Business Transfers: In the unlikely event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, no user data would be transferred because no user data is collected.

Data Retention and Deletion

Because we do not collect any personal data, there is nothing for us to retain or delete. Data on your own device can be cleared at any time by removing devices in the App or deleting the App itself.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not collect any personal data from anyone, including children.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. You are encouraged to review this policy periodically.
