Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

Introduction

Silenzio (the "App") is developed and operated by Matteo Crippa ("we", "us", "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

The App identifies and silences spam callers using Apple's Call Directory extension together with a community-maintained blocklist of phone numbers reported as spam. Privacy is a core design principle — the App requires no account, collects no personal data, and checks incoming calls entirely on your device.

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

Information We Collect

We do not collect personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number, and the App requires no account, sign-in, or registration.

Information Stored on Your Device

The App stores the following data locally, in its own sandbox and in a shared app group container used by the Call Directory extension:

  • Community blocklist: The list of phone numbers reported as spam, downloaded from our API and stored in a local SQLite database
  • Pending reports: Reports you submit are stored locally before they are synchronized
  • Session identifier: A randomly generated identifier (a UUID) created on your device the first time you use the App, used only to prevent duplicate reports and to rate-limit abuse. It is not linked to your name, email, Apple ID, or any device identifier (see below)
  • App preferences: Your settings, such as whether the optional contacts whitelist is enabled (stored in UserDefaults)

All of this local data is removed when you delete the App.

Information Sent to Our Server

When you report a phone number as spam, the App sends the following to our API (hosted on Cloudflare Workers):

DataPurpose
Reported phone numberAdded to the community blocklist so other users are protected
Random session identifier (UUID)Prevent duplicate reports for the same number and rate-limit abuse
Report timestampAggregation and confidence scoring
Confidence valueRanking and display of the blocklist

The session identifier is generated randomly on your device, is not derived from any hardware or advertising identifier, and cannot be used by us to identify you. It is deleted from your device when you uninstall the App. Reports are pseudonymous: we cannot link a report back to your identity.

We do not store your IP address, your contacts, your call history, or any information about who calls you, when they call, or how often.

Contacts (Optional)

If you enable the optional contacts whitelist, the App reads the phone numbers in your address book to make sure the people you know are never blocked. This happens entirely on your device, inside the Call Directory extension:

  • Only phone numbers are read — never names, emails, or other contact fields
  • Contact numbers are never transmitted to us or any third party, and are not stored in our blocklist
  • The whitelist is optional and off by default; you can disable it at any time in Settings or by revoking Contacts permission in iOS Settings
  • You can deny Contacts access and the App remains fully functional

Information We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect, store, or share:

  • Name, email, phone number, or any contact information belonging to you
  • Accounts or credentials — the App does not require sign-in or registration
  • Device identifiers (IDFA, advertising ID, vendor ID, etc.)
  • Your IP address (we do not log or store it)
  • Location data or GPS coordinates
  • Call logs, or any information about who you call or who calls you
  • Your contacts (except the optional, on-device whitelist described above)
  • Browsing history or behavioral data
  • Payment or financial information — the App is free
  • Photos, media, health, or biometric data
  • Usage analytics, crash reports, or telemetry

How the Call Directory Extension Works

Apple manages the Call Directory extension system. When a call comes in, iOS checks the number against our local blocklist on your device:

  • The Call Directory extension reads the local SQLite blocklist shared through an app group container
  • No network access is needed or performed during calls — the incoming number never leaves your phone
  • We never learn which numbers called you, how many calls you received, or which entries were matched

How We Use Your Information

  • To operate and improve the community blocklist of spam numbers
  • To aggregate user reports and compute a confidence score per number
  • To prevent duplicate reports for the same number
  • To rate-limit and mitigate abuse of our API
  • To enable the optional contacts whitelist on your device

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling, marketing, or any purpose unrelated to the App's functionality. We do not sell your information.

Legal Basis for Processing

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), we rely on the following legal bases:

  • Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) — Operating the community blocklist to protect users from spam calls. The reported numbers are those of the spam callers, not of our users, and reports are pseudonymous.
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) — We access your Contacts only after you grant explicit permission through the iOS permissions dialog and enable the optional whitelist. You may withdraw consent at any time by disabling the whitelist or revoking permission in iOS Settings.

How We Share Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information, and no personal data is collected. The only data leaving your device is the pseudonymous report data described above.

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

The App does not use cookies or any form of tracking technology. There are no web views, no cookie storage, no advertising identifiers, and no fingerprinting mechanisms.

Data Security

We implement the following measures to protect your information:

  • On-device processing: Incoming calls are checked against the local blocklist and Contacts are read locally
  • Encrypted network requests: All communication with our API uses HTTPS
  • App Sandbox & App Group: Data is confined to the App's sandbox and a shared app group container accessible only to the App and its Call Directory extension
  • Data minimization: Only the minimum data necessary for each feature is stored or sent
  • No personal data at rest on our servers: Our database holds spam numbers and pseudonymous session identifiers, not personal data

No system is 100% secure, but because no personal data leaves your device, there is no personal data on our servers to breach.

Your Rights

Under GDPR and applicable privacy laws, you have the following rights:

  • Access, correction, deletion, portability — All data about you resides on your device. You can view it in the App and delete it via Settings > Clear Database, or by uninstalling the App. For reported numbers or session identifiers held on our servers, contact us using the form below
  • Withdraw consent — Disable the contacts whitelist in Settings or revoke Contacts permission in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Contacts
  • Restrict processing — You may use the App without reporting any number; the App remains functional
  • Object — You may object to processing by ceasing use of the App
  • Lodge a complaint — You have the right to lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority (Garante per la protezione dei dati personali) or your local supervisory authority

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the information below. We will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law.
