Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 28, 2026

Introduction

VoltMinder (the "App") is developed and operated by Matteo Crippa ("we", "us", "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

The App provides live EV battery telemetry monitoring on your iPhone and CarPlay, connecting to your vehicle via a Bluetooth ELM327 OBD-II adapter. Privacy is a core design principle — the App processes all sensitive data on-device by default, and any data sharing is opt-in.

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

Information We Collect

Location Data

The App uses your device's GPS location to calculate ETA, show nearby charging stations, and estimate arrival State of Charge during navigation. Location processing happens entirely on your device:

  • Your GPS coordinates are used locally to query nearby charging points from Open Charge Map and calculate route information via Apple MapKit
  • Coordinates are never transmitted to any VoltMinder-operated server
  • No location data is stored on any backend

Location is accessed using "When In Use" permission only.

Bluetooth Data

The App uses Bluetooth Low Energy (BLE) to communicate with an ELM327 OBD-II adapter connected to your vehicle's OBD2 port. This connection streams live battery telemetry (State of Charge, voltage, current, temperatures, etc.) to your iPhone:

  • Bluetooth communication occurs entirely on your device — between your iPhone and the OBD-II adapter
  • No vehicle telemetry data is transmitted to any server by default
  • The paired dongle's identifier is stored locally for automatic reconnection

Calendar Data (Optional)

If you grant Calendar access, the App may infer how long you will stay at a destination to help plan charging stops. This data is processed locally on your device:

  • Calendar event durations are read solely to estimate parking duration
  • Calendar data is never transmitted off your device
  • You can deny Calendar access and the App remains fully functional

Siri / App Intents

The App integrates with Siri so you can start battery-aware navigation using voice commands. When you use Siri, Apple processes your voice input according to Apple's Privacy Policy. VoltMinder receives only the resolved destination.

Information We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect, store, or share:

  • Name, email, phone number, or any contact information
  • Device identifiers (IDFA, advertising ID, vendor ID, etc.)
  • Browsing history or behavioral data
  • Payment or financial information — all StoreKit transactions are processed by Apple
  • Account credentials — the App does not require an account, sign-in, or registration
  • Photos, contacts (except Calendar, as described above), or media
  • Health or biometric data
  • Usage analytics, crash reports, or telemetry

How We Use Your Information

  • To connect to your vehicle via Bluetooth and display live battery telemetry
  • To display your position on the map and find nearby charging stations
  • To calculate route energy consumption and suggest charging stops
  • To provide CarPlay Dashboard widgets and a full-screen HUD with battery metrics
  • To log trips locally for efficiency analytics
  • To send local notifications when battery State of Charge drops below a threshold you configure
  • To provide Siri voice control for battery queries and navigation (optional)

Legal Basis for Processing

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), we process your personal information on the following legal bases:

  • Consent — We access your device's location only after you grant explicit permission through the iOS location permissions dialog. We access Calendar only after you grant explicit permission. You may withdraw consent at any time by changing permissions in your device Settings.
  • Legitimate Interest — Bluetooth communication with your OBD-II adapter is necessary for the core functionality of the App. No consent is required for local BLE communication, as it does not involve processing of personal data by us.

Your vehicle telemetry and location are processed entirely on-device. No data is transmitted to us or any third party by default, so no other legal basis for processing applies.

How We Share Your Information

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with anyone. By default, no data leaves your device.

Opt-In Data Sharing

You may choose to enable the following optional features, each of which involves data leaving your device:

1. iCloud Family Sharing (CloudKit) — If you purchase the Family Plan and opt into sharing, computed trip logs are synced across family members via Apple's CloudKit using end-to-end encryption. Only computed TripLog records (distance, efficiency, SoC range, temperatures) are synced. Apple manages the encryption — VoltMinder cannot read your family's data.

2. EVNotify Integration — If you explicitly enable EVNotify upload and provide your credentials (akey/token), the App sends telemetry data (SoC, SoH, power, voltage, current) to EVNotify's servers (https://evnotify.de). This is opt-in and off by default.

3. Leaderboard — If you opt in, anonymous efficiency data (Wh/km, vehicle ID, a randomly generated driver UUID) is sent to our Cloudflare Workers API. No personal identifiers are included. You can delete your leaderboard entry at any time via Settings.

4. Community Profiles & Charge Curves — If you submit a community profile or charge curve, the vehicle data you provide is sent to our Cloudflare Workers API. No personal data is included in submissions.

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

The App does not use cookies or any form of tracking technology. There are no web views, no cookie storage, and no fingerprinting mechanisms.

Data Security

All data processing is performed locally on your device. We implement the following measures to protect your information:

  • On-device processing: Vehicle telemetry, location, and calendar data are processed locally and never transmitted by default
  • Encrypted network requests: All network requests use HTTPS
  • App Sandbox: iOS sandboxing prevents unauthorized access by other applications
  • Data minimization: Only the minimum data necessary for each feature is accessed
  • End-to-end encryption: CloudKit family sync uses Apple's end-to-end encryption
  • No backend storage: We operate no database of user data

No system is 100% secure, but because no personal data leaves your device by default, there is no server-side data to breach.

All data resides on your device and is removed when you uninstall the App. For opt-in data (leaderboard, community submissions), use the in-app deletion option or contact us

To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the information below. We will respond within the timeframe required by applicable law.

International Data Transfers

No personal data is transferred across borders by default. If you opt into:

In all cases, international transfers are governed by the EU Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) or the Data Privacy Framework (DPF), as applicable to each provider.
