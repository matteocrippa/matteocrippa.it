Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 3, 2026

Introduction

Meteo Lombardia (the "App") is developed and operated by Matteo Crippa ("we", "us", "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application, together with a detailed description of the third-party data the App presents.

The App is an Italian-language weather companion for Lombardy. It turns raw meteorological data into conversational, human-friendly advice: a "Today" dashboard (current conditions, 12-hour outlook, gear suggestions, air quality and pollen), a "Weekend" escape recommender that scans curated Lombardy day-trip destinations, a live rain radar map, and local rain alerts. Privacy is a core design principle — the App has no account, no analytics, and no backend of ours; the only data it ever sends out is your approximate coordinates to the third-party services it queries for weather information.

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

Information We Collect

Location Data

The App uses your device's approximate location to fetch weather, air quality, and radar data for your area and to rank weekend destinations by distance. Location processing is minimal:

  • Your coordinates are read with "When In Use" permission only, at roughly 1 km accuracy
  • Your coordinates are sent to Open-Meteo (forecast and air quality APIs) solely to request the weather for your position; they are not logged, stored, or associated with any identifier by us
  • Your coordinates are also used locally by Apple Maps (MapKit) to center the radar map and by Apple's geocoder to display your town name
  • No location data is ever sent to any server we operate (we operate none), and nothing is tracked in the background

If you deny location permission, the App falls back to a default Lombardy location so core features still work.

Information We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect, store, or share:

  • Name, email, phone number, or any contact information
  • Device identifiers (IDFA, advertising ID, vendor ID, etc.)
  • Browsing history or behavioral data
  • Payment or financial information
  • Account credentials — the App does not require an account, sign-in, or registration
  • Photos, contacts, calendar, or media
  • Health or biometric data
  • Usage analytics, crash reports, or telemetry

How We Use Your Information

  • To fetch the weather forecast for your approximate location from Open-Meteo
  • To fetch air quality and pollen data from the Open-Meteo air quality API (CAMS)
  • To display your position on the radar map and rank nearby weekend destinations
  • To schedule local rain notifications entirely on your device

Legal Basis for Processing

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), we process your personal information on the following legal basis:

  • Consent — We access your device's location only after you grant explicit permission through the iOS location permissions dialog. You may withdraw consent at any time by changing location permissions in your device Settings.

Your location is used only to obtain the information you asked the App for (the weather where you are). It is not retained, combined, or used for any other purpose. Since we operate no backend, no other legal basis for processing applies.

How We Share Your Information

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with anyone. The App never sends data to any server we operate.

  • Third-Party Services: The only data that leaves your device is your approximate coordinates, sent directly to the weather data providers described below so they can return the data the App displays.
  • Legal Compliance: We may disclose information only if required to do so by law or in response to valid legal process. Since we collect no personal data on any backend, there is nothing meaningful to disclose.
  • Business Transfers: In the unlikely event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, no user data would be transferred because no user data is collected.

Data Disclaimer

Please read this section carefully.

Third-party data. All weather forecasts, radar imagery, air quality values, and pollen counts shown in the App are provided by third-party services (Open-Meteo, MeteoSwiss, ItaliaMeteo/ARPAE, DWD, CAMS, and RainViewer) and are model outputs — estimates, not measurements or guarantees. Meteorological models are probabilistic, update frequently, and can disagree with one another or with reality, especially at long ranges and in rapidly evolving conditions.

Automated advice. The conversational messages, gear suggestions (umbrella, jacket, sunglasses, and similar), and weekend destination recommendations are generated automatically by simple rule-based logic applied to the model data described above. They are not professional meteorological, health, or travel advice. They may differ from official forecasts or from what actually happens, and they should never be the only basis for decisions that affect your safety, health, or travel plans.

Air quality and pollen. Air quality index and pollen values are model estimates and may not reflect local conditions. They are informational only and must not be treated as medical or health advice.

Safety. The App is not a severe-weather warning service. In case of storms, floods, heatwaves, avalanches, or any potentially dangerous weather, always consult the official sources: ARPA Lombardia, MeteoSwiss, the Italian Civil Protection (Protezione Civile), and the local authorities. Mountain excursions in particular must be planned against official mountain-weather reports.

Limitation of liability. To the maximum extent permitted by applicable law, we are not liable for any loss or damage arising from your reliance on the data or the automated advice shown in the App, including decisions to postpone, change, or undertake activities based on it. The App is provided "as is" and without warranty of accuracy, completeness, or timeliness.

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

The App does not use cookies or any form of tracking technology. There are no web views, no cookie storage, and no fingerprinting mechanisms.

Data Security

All network requests to the data providers use HTTPS. Location is read at coarse (kilometer) accuracy, used only for the query, and never stored by us. Because the App operates no backend and stores no personal data, there is no server-side data that could be breached.

  • On-device processing: All advice generation, notification scheduling, and caching happen locally on your device
  • Encrypted network requests: All requests to third-party services use HTTPS
  • App Sandbox: iOS sandboxing prevents unauthorized access by other applications
  • Data minimization: Only the minimum data necessary (coarse coordinates) is sent, and only while the App is in use

No system is 100% secure, but because no personal data leaves your device beyond a coarse coordinate query, the exposure is minimal.
