Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

Introduction

Notturno (the "App") is developed and operated by Matteo Crippa ("we", "us", "our"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our application.

The App is a Safari web extension that applies dark mode to the web pages you visit in Safari. Privacy is a core design principle — the App requires no account, has no server, collects no personal data, and processes every page entirely on your Mac.

By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

Information We Collect

We do not collect personal information such as your name, email address, or phone number, and the App requires no account, sign-in, or registration.

Why the App Needs Webpage Access

Dark mode has to be applied to a page itself, so the App needs Safari's permission to read and modify webpages. That access is used for one purpose: to read the structure and colours of the page you are currently looking at, and to restyle it. Nothing else is done with it.

Page content is read in the page as it renders, recoloured locally, and discarded. The App has no code path that copies page content anywhere.

Information Stored on Your Mac

The App stores the following data locally, in Safari's own extension storage and in the page's own local storage:

  • Your switches: Whether Notturno is on for every website, and any per-site exceptions you have set (for example, turning it off for one site)
  • Page performance records: If a page has previously made Notturno misbehave, a record of that page and the version of the engine that failed, so the App does not repeat the same failure on your next visit. Removing the App deletes it
  • A per-site decision cache: A short-lived marker written into the page's own storage so the App can decide whether to paint before the page finishes loading, which prevents a flash of light content. This is the one item Notturno writes into storage it does not own, and it is cleared by clearing Safari's website data for the site, not by uninstalling the App

The App's own data is removed when you delete the App. The per-site decision cache above is the one exception, and it is removed by clearing Safari's website data for that site.

What Leaves Your Mac

Nothing. The App contains no networking code. It does not upload, transmit, synchronise, or share anything — not page content, not browsing history, not the URLs you visit, not analytics, not crash reports.

Information We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect, store, or share:

  • Name, email, phone number, or any contact information belonging to you
  • Accounts or credentials — the App does not require sign-in or registration
  • The URLs you visit, or any record of your browsing history
  • The content of pages you view, including text, images, and form data
  • Device identifiers (IDFA, advertising ID, vendor ID, etc.)
  • Your IP address (we do not log, receive, or store it — the App never contacts us)
  • Location data or GPS coordinates
  • Usage analytics, crash reports, telemetry, or diagnostics
  • Advertising or marketing data
  • Cookies, web views, or fingerprinting
  • Payment or financial information — the App is free

How the App Works

The App is a rendering engine that runs inside Safari. For each page you visit, entirely on your device:

  • It reads the page's structure and the colours it uses, to understand what the page is made of
  • It decides whether the page already has a dark mode worth using. If it does, the App steps aside and changes nothing
  • Otherwise it transforms the page — its own colours, its backgrounds, its text — keeping photographs as photographs and keeping colours that carry meaning, such as a price or a stock indicator, recognisable
  • If the page starts behaving badly, the App switches itself off for that page rather than leaving you with something broken

All of this happens locally, in your browser, as you browse.

How We Use Your Information

  • To operate and improve the App's rendering on the pages you visit
  • To remember your per-site preferences
  • To avoid repeating a rendering failure on a page that has already failed

We do not use your information for advertising, profiling, marketing, or any purpose unrelated to the App's functionality. We do not sell your information.

Legal Basis for Processing

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), we rely on the following legal bases:

  • Legitimate interest (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) — Operating the App on your device so the pages you visit are readable in dark mode. The processing is local to your Mac and no personal data is transmitted to us.
  • Consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) — We read webpages only after you grant Safari's extension permission and enable Notturno. You may withdraw this at any time by disabling the extension in Safari > Settings > Extensions.

How We Share Your Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information, and no personal data is collected. There is no personal data to share.

Third-Party Services

The App contains no third-party analytics, crash reporting, advertising, or tracking SDKs. Specifically: no Firebase, no Sentry, no Crashlytics, no Google Analytics, no Amplitude, no Mixpanel, no AdMob, no social media SDKs, no payment processors, and no remote configuration or A/B testing frameworks.

The App also declares no networking entitlement, so it is not capable of making network requests at all.

We may disclose information only if required to do so by law or in response to valid legal process. In the unlikely event of a merger, acquisition, or sale of assets, no personal user data would be transferred because no personal user data is collected.

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

The App uses no cookies or any form of tracking technology. There are no web views, no cookie storage, no advertising identifiers, and no fingerprinting mechanisms. A small marker may be written into a page's own local storage for technical reasons described above; it is not a cookie, it is not readable by other apps, and it carries no identifier.

Data Security

We implement the following measures to protect your information:

  • On-device processing: Pages are read and recoloured entirely within Safari, on your Mac
  • No network capability: The App ships with networking disabled at the build level, so it cannot transmit data even if it wanted to
  • Data minimisation: The App stores only your switches and the two short records described above
  • No personal data at rest: Because nothing is transmitted, there is no personal data on any server of ours to breach

No system is 100% secure, but because no personal data leaves your device, there is no personal data on our servers to breach.
